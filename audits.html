01 · Overview

$GHS is a standard Solana Token-2022 with no custom, fund-moving smart contract in the trading path. Authorities are renounced, the token has no tax / hook / delegate, and the site and backend follow a non-custodial, signature-authenticated, least-privilege design.

Bottom line: there is no mechanism by which the token or the site can seize, freeze, or redirect a holder's assets. Overall security posture: strong.

03 · Non-custodial by design

• Private keys never leave your wallet (Phantom / Solflare). The site holds no keys and takes no custody.
• The 3D church authenticates you with a read-only signed message, never a transfer approval — connecting cannot move funds.
• The owner funding tool signs its transaction inside your own wallet; no key or secret is ever sent to a server.

04 · Application & backend controls

• Authenticated privileged actions — admin/broadcast commands are verified server-side with Ed25519 signatures, a single-use nonce (anti-replay) and a ±120s freshness window. They cannot be forged, replayed, or issued from a browser.
• Server-side RPC proxy with a method allow-list — RPC credentials stay on the server, never in client code.
• Least-privilege database — Row-Level Security: public data is read-only; all writes go through authenticated Edge Functions.
• Transport & browser hardening — CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, and related headers enforced.
• Responsible disclosure — /.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116); continuous secret scanning & dependency monitoring in CI.

05 · Community-rewards integrity (Manna)

Weekly Manna is distributed as a strictly equal share to the verified congregation, with the distribution's Merkle root recorded on-chain for independent verification. The mechanism runs on an immutable on-chain program (manna_vault) where the equal-split rule is enforced by the program itself and each distribution emits an on-chain proof. The program is validated on devnet ahead of mainnet, with source published for open review.
